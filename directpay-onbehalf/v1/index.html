Make a payment on behalf of a taxpayer

Pay an individual's or business's federal taxes from a bank account. No account is required. Signing in lets us fill in details you've used before.

Sign in to save time. We'll fill in the payer and taxpayer details from your account. You can also continue without signing in.

1 Who is making this payment?

This is the payer of record. Businesses and financial institutions are not asked for a personal Social Security number.

2 What are you paying?

Start from a notice you received, or make a general payment. Individual (IMF) and business (BMF) tax types are both supported.

I have a noticeEnter the notice number and date from your letter.
General paymentChoose a reason and, if you know it, a tax period.

3 Whose tax account is this for?

Full information for the person or business you're paying for — this is the account the payment applies to.

4 Amount & date

Enter the amount and the date you want the bank debit to occur.

5 Bank account

Payments come directly from a checking or savings account. There is no processing fee.
