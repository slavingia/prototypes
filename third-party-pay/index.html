Payments · Pay on behalf of a taxpayer

Make a payment on behalf of a taxpayer

Respond to an IRS levy or pay toward a federal tax lien for someone else — one same-day payment for one taxpayer, from a bank account. No IRS account or sign-in is required.

All fields are required unless marked optional.

1 Who is making this payment?

This is the payer of record. We only ask who you are and how to reach you — no Social Security number is needed to pay for someone else.

2 What is this payment for?

Choose the document you received or are acting on. Individual (IMF) and business (BMF) accounts are both supported.

Notice of levyForm 668-A, 668-W, or 668-B — send the payment with the completed levy response.
Federal tax lienPay toward a Notice of Federal Tax Lien — for example at a sale, closing, or refinance.

3 Whose tax account is this for?

Any payer can pay for a person or a business — a bank answering a wage levy pays for an individual; a family member can pay for a family business.

Full information for the person or business you're paying for — this is the account the payment applies to.

Identity verified — TIN and name control match IRS records We check the TIN and name control against IRS records before you enter payment details. Account balances are never shown.

4 Amount

Enter the amount you're sending. Payments are same-day: the bank debit is initiated today, when you submit.

5 Bank account

Payments come directly from a checking or savings account. There is no processing fee.

Bank account (ACH) — no fee
Certification
