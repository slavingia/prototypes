Issue #10 · Variant 3

IRS mobile app — wallet-first, chat-first

The app opens on a conversation: ask a question (or tap a chip like “Check my refund status”) and the omnibar turns into a chat. Account data is gated: as a guest you can’t see your balance, records, or notices — the assistant explains why and offers to sign in. The one guest-accessible task is Where’s My Refund, because that flow verifies your identity as it goes. And “Make a payment” never assumes you mean your balance — it asks what kind (balance due, estimated tax, extension, amended return, notice, or a plan) first.
