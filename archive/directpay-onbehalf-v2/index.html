Make a payment on behalf of a taxpayer

One page, no account required. Enter the taxpayer's full information, choose what you're paying, and pay from a bank account (ACH). The panel on the right shows exactly when the payment is expected to post.

1 Payment type

Individual (IMF) and business (BMF) taxes are both supported. Choosing here sets the taxpayer fields and posting timeline.

2 Taxpayer information

Full information for the person or business you're paying for — this is who the payment posts to.

3 Amount

Enter the amount and the date you want the ACH debit to occur.

4 Bank account (ACH)

Payments come directly from a checking or savings account — the only method, and always free.
