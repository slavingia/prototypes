What may change

Your refund amount may be reduced to pay a past due debt, such as child support or a federal or state debt. If this happens, you'll get a notice from the Bureau of the Fiscal Service (BFS), which issues IRS refunds. The notice will provide the debt amount and the BFS contact information. The details of the debt offset aren't provided to the IRS.

Learn about reduced refunds

If you didn't receive your refund

Request a refund trace. You can file a request with Form 3911, Taxpayer Statement Regarding Refund, or by phone. We're here Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.
